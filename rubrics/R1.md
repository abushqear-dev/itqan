# R1 Claim support (Discovery, D-S6)

Version: 1. Read rubrics/COMMON.md first.

Sample at least 10 claims, weighted to table-stakes and differentiation claims. Fetch each cited page and judge one claim at a time.

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | The page states the claim directly, for the named product, and is current within the freshness window. |
| 3 | The page supports the claim only partly or indirectly (e.g. a feature is mentioned but its scope is unclear), or it is a secondary source. |
| 1 | The page does not support the claim, contradicts it, is unreachable, or is about another product. |

Metric value: the share of claims at level ≥ 4.
