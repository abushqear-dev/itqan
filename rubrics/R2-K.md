# R2-K Checkable principles (Constitution, K-S1)

Per MUST principle:

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | A named later check, test or review step can show a violation. |
| 3 | It is checkable in principle, but nothing in the pipeline is named to check it. |
| 1 | An aspiration with no observable violation ("high quality", "user-friendly"). |

Metric value: the share of principles at level ≥ 4.
