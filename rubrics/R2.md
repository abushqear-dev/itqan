# R2 Requirement quality, ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 (Product Spec, P-S7)

Version: 1. Read rubrics/COMMON.md first.

Judge each acceptance criterion on the five characteristics a script cannot check: **necessary, appropriate, feasible, correct, unambiguous in context.** The level is the lowest of the five.

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Traces to a stated need. Describes what, not how. Feasible with the Constitution's constraints. Agrees with the inputs and the glossary. A tester and a developer would read it the same way. |
| 3 | One weakness: a design detail leaks in, a term is used loosely, or a reasonable second reading exists that the Example does not rule out. |
| 1 | Not needed by any input, contradicts an input or another criterion, cannot be built under the constraints, or has two readings that lead to different behaviour. |
