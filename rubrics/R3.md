# R3 INVEST (Product Spec, P-S8)

Version: 1. Read rubrics/COMMON.md first.

Judge each story on **Independent, Negotiable, Valuable** (Estimable, Small and Testable are scripted). The level is the lowest of the three. Published LLM-vs-expert agreement here is only ρ ≈ 0.53-0.65, and models score lower than experts, so this rubric needs the most anchors.

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Can be built and demonstrated without another unfinished story. States the need, not the implementation. Its "so that" names a benefit for the role that the inputs care about. |
| 3 | Depends on one other story for its demo, or prescribes one implementation detail, or the benefit is generic ("so that I can use the app"). |
| 1 | Cannot be shown on its own, is a technical task in story form, or has no benefit for any role in the inputs. |
