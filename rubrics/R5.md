# R5 Threat model quality (Technical Design, T-S1)

Version: 1. Read rubrics/COMMON.md first.

Per trust boundary:

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Threats are specific to this product's data and roles (e.g. "volunteer reads another volunteer's hours via `/api/hours?userId=`"). The mitigation is concrete and testable, and linked to an AC, an ASVS row and a task. |
| 3 | The threats are correct but generic ("injection", "XSS") with standard mitigations, and the links are present but the test is vague. |
| 1 | A category is listed with no threat, or a mitigation cannot be tested, or a known product risk (for example the role table's sensitive actions) is missing. |
