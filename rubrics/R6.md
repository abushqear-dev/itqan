# R6 Decision records (Technical Design, T-S2)

Version: 1. Read rubrics/COMMON.md first.

Per non-obvious decision (stack, data store, auth, state management, anything a reviewer would ask about):

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | An ADR exists with context, at least two real options, a decision tied to a QAS or constraint, and its consequences, including the negative ones. |
| 3 | An ADR exists, but the options are a straw man or the consequences are only positive. |
| 1 | No ADR, or it restates the decision without a reason. |
