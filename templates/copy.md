# Copy deck: <product name>

Every user-facing message, written once. Screens (UX Spec), the error model (Technical Design), the build and the tests refer to these keys. Wording follows the glossary; placeholders are in braces.

## Success and confirmations
- `MSG.EXAMPLE_DONE`: <You are signed up for {shift}.>

## Errors (with the fix)
- `MSG.EXAMPLE_INVALID`: <Enter a number of spots from 1 to 100.>

## Empty states
- `MSG.EXAMPLE_EMPTY`: <No shifts this week. Published shifts will appear here.>

## Confirmations before destructive actions
- `MSG.EXAMPLE_CONFIRM`: <Cancel your spot on {shift}? Someone on the waitlist may take it.>

## Labels and plurals
- `MSG.SPOTS_FREE`: <{count, plural, one {# spot free} other {# spots free}}>
